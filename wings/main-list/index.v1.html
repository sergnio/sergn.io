Issue #83 · design directions

Wings and locations

Three takes on the buffalo list, a wing page and a location page. Steve O's is the example spot. Each file shows desktop and mobile; resize the window to see mobile.

What's settled

  • URLs: /wings/steve-os and /wings/steve-os/big-boy-buffalo.
  • Wings get letter grades. Locations don't.
  • The main list holds every buffalo wing, plus one "best non-buffalo" wing for spots without buffalo, marked with an asterisk and a footnote.
  • Other wings show only on their location page.
  • The #90 "All wings" toggle gets a dashed placeholder slot only.
  • Rules follow the wings constitution.

Directions

  1. A · Evolved cards

    Closest to today's site. Podium plus rows on the list, a photo hero on the wing page with a "spot" card and sibling list, and a card grid on the location page.

    All screensListWingLocation

  2. B · Menu ledger

    Typographic and narrow, like a printed menu. Big rank numerals, dotted leaders and red grades. The wing page ends with a menu card listing everything tried at the spot.

    All screensListWingLocation

  3. C · Tier board

    Photo-forward and grouped by tier letter (S, A, B). Full-bleed photo hero on the wing page with a dark location panel, and a dark stats band on the location page.

    All screensListWingLocation

My pick

A's structure, with B's menu card on the wing page. A fits the rest of the site and scales as wings are added. B's "everything I've tried here" card makes the location link feel like part of the review instead of a sidebar. C's tier letters are fun but repeat what each grade already says.

Open questions

  • What should the main list be called, since it's "buffalo" but holds an asterisk wing?
  • On a location page, show main-list rank (#3 on the list) or a rank within the spot? The constitution says rank follows the list showing.
  • Most spots have no website on file. Show a map link instead, or hide the row?
  • Keep the 👑 for S+?
  • Should the main list show visit counts, or keep them on wing and location pages only?
  • What should a wing with no photo show? The mocks use a striped placeholder.
