# Wings Constitution

The rules for how wings are judged, listed, and graded on [sergn.io/wings](https://sergn.io/wings).

## Core Principles

### I. The wing comes first

- A wing MUST be judged on eating it: juicy, crispy, tender, and not dry.
- Size MUST be noted, but it counts against a wing only when it hurts the cooking (dry, rubbery, or undercooked inside). Big is fine.
- The sauce MUST NOT carry a weak wing. A tasty, sugary sauce does not make up for a dry or soggy wing.

### II. Flavor decides, price breaks ties

- Flavor counts most; crunch, juiciness, and size count equally after it. Heat counts only as part of flavor.
- Price MUST only break ties between wings that are otherwise even. It MUST NOT lift a wing past a clearly better one.
- Service and atmosphere MUST NOT change a grade or rank. They belong in the notes.

Rationale: the list once mixed value with flavor, which misled readers ([#87](https://github.com/sergnio/sergn.io/issues/87)).

### III. The S-tier gate

- A wing MUST have both a good wing and a good sauce to reach S tier. Missing either caps it at A+.
- A non-buffalo sauce MUST be done really well to pass: not overly salty, sugary, or fatty. This is a judgment call.
- The letter grade follows the ranking: every S ranks above every A, and rank orders wings within a letter.

### IV. Buffalo is the benchmark

- Buffalo means the cayenne, vinegar, and butter family of sauces, judged by flavor, whatever the menu calls it (Hot, Original Hot, Spicy Buffalo, and so on).
- The main wings list MUST show every buffalo wing. A place can have more than one, for example two wing types.
- A place with no buffalo MUST put its single best wing on the main list, marked with an asterisk. An asterisk wing can still reach S tier.
- Other flavors MUST NOT appear on the main list. They appear on their location's page.

### V. One entry per wing

- Every flavor and wing type tried MUST be its own entry, with its own grade.
- Entries at the same place MUST share one location record ([#84](https://github.com/sergnio/sergn.io/issues/84)).
- The same rules grade every entry, on the main list or not.

## Presentation

- The asterisk is explained in a footnote on the page, not a hover.
- An optional "All wings" view may show every entry in one ranking ([#90](https://github.com/sergnio/sergn.io/issues/90)). Rank numbers follow whichever list is showing.
- The one-line ranking summary on the wings page is drawn from this document ([#88](https://github.com/sergnio/sergn.io/issues/88)).

## Governance

- Sergio amends this document. Every amendment updates the version and the Last Amended date.
- Versioning: MAJOR when a rule is removed or redefined, MINOR when a rule or section is added or expanded, PATCH for wording only.
- Every new or edited wing entry MUST be checked against these rules before publishing, and a rule change MUST be followed by a re-rank of affected entries.
